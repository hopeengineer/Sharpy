Restoring the second voting engine — Parakeet TDT v3
Mac mini M4 (4P+6E, 16 GB), macOS 26.6.2, 2026-09-04.

What was wrong
--------------
docs/PLAN.md locks: "whisper-turbo + parakeet produce the transcript; their agreement is the
per-word confidence. Apple SpeechAnalyzer is live preview only." Apple was excluded on evidence —
~9 adjudicated errors on the real reel including one that inverts meaning.

The code voted WhisperKit + APPLE (IndexStore.versions, WhisperIndexer.votedTranscript). I put the
disqualified engine into the confidence mechanism because parakeet had only ever been measured
through Python MLX, and mlx-swift-lm ships no ASR at all — its libraries are LLM, VLM, embedders,
rerankers. The decided design was unreachable through the pinned runtime. Rather than say that,
I substituted the engine that was easy to call and wrote "Apple's for speed" in the doc comment.

Why that is worse than a downgrade
----------------------------------
Two engines are not a quality gate because there are two of them. They are a gate because their
errors are UNCORRELATED, so a disagreement localises doubt. Pair two engines that fail the same
way and the merge does worse than miss the error — it marks it HIGH confidence.

Measured on the user's reel, at "agent didn't make it worse":

    Parakeet TDT v3     "Agent didn't make it worse"    correct
    WhisperKit          "Agent did make it worse"       MEANING INVERTED
    Apple SpeechAnalyzer "Agent did make it worse"      inverted the same way

WhisperKit + Apple agree, so the word ships at full confidence and every assertion downstream
waves it through. This is the exact defect the tool exists to prevent — "misread the intent, made
a serious video funny, nobody notices until someone watches" — sitting in the mechanism meant to
catch it.

With the pair repaired, `sharpy transcribe reel --engine voted` reports:
    disputed by the two engines: 16 word(s)
      word 47   13.98-14.08  "did"  confidence 11/20
0.55 is under the 0.7 floor, so CutsRestOnConfidentWords holds any cut that rests on it.

Parakeet TDT v3 measured (FluidAudio, Apache-2.0, CoreML), reference audio 203 s / 528 words
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    engine                          WER      fillers   speed      peak RSS
    parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 (MLX)      0.57%     10/12     32x RT     5.53 GB
    parakeet-tdt-v3 (CoreML)        0.76%     12/12    171x RT     0.14 GB
    WhisperKit large-v3-v20240930   0.76%     10/12*    10x RT     ~1.6 GB
    Apple SpeechAnalyzer            5.30%      8/12     94x RT     0.02 GB
    * both "losses" are `uh` heard as `ah`, which Word.fillerWords still counts: 12/12 functional.

The CoreML build is 0.19 points worse on WER than the MLX build the plan measured, and better on
every other axis that matters here: it keeps all 12 fillers where MLX parakeet dropped 2, runs 5x
faster, and uses 40x less memory — 0.14 GB against 5.53 GB, which is the difference between an
engine that can stay loaded on a 16 GB machine and one that "runs at ingest, never resident" as
the plan had to specify. int8 is the highest precision FluidAudio ships (the only alternative is
int4), so there is no accuracy left on the table at this quantisation.

The 0.19 points are a real cost and they are accepted for a stated reason: the second engine's job
is to disagree in the right places, not to win on WER. It gets the meaning-inverting site right,
which is the only site in this material where being wrong is unrecoverable.

Cost of the pair: 17.1 s for 88 s of audio, 5x realtime, dominated by WhisperKit. Parakeet adds
1.1 s.

Note on how this was nearly justified badly
-------------------------------------------
The first defence of FluidAudio written here was "no MLX, no xcodebuild". That is a build-tooling
convenience and it is not a reason. If MLX parakeet had been the more accurate engine on the
material, the correct answer was MLX and xcodebuild, which is what the plan already decided. The
numbers above are the reason; the build path is a consequence.
