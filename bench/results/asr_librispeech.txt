ASR on real speech — LibriSpeech test-clean and test-other
Mac mini M4 (4P+6E, 16 GB), macOS 26.6.2, 2026-09-04.

Why this exists
---------------
Every ASR number in this repo came from 528 words of `say` output and one 88 s reel. That is the
same fixture mistake that produced three wrong verdicts in diarization, sitting unexamined in the
ASR path — and it was already distorting a decision. The "0.19 WER points" separating MLX parakeet
(0.57%) from the CoreML build (0.76%) is, on 528 words, EXACTLY ONE WORD:

    0.57% x 528 = 3.01 errors
    0.76% x 528 = 4.01 errors

A 5.53 GB runtime was nearly built to recover one word of synthetic speech.

Corpora: LibriSpeech test-clean (2620 utterances, 53 029 words) and test-other (2939 utterances,
52 884 words) — 105 913 reference words, 200x the previous evidence. Scored with Whisper's
EnglishTextNormalizer, so the number measures mis-hearing rather than formatting convention;
missing and empty hypotheses count as full loss rather than being skipped.

                     test-clean          test-other         overall        speed    failures
    Parakeet TDT v3     2.29%               3.85%            3.06%       92-99x RT      1
    WhisperKit          2.02%               4.29%            3.15%        7-8x RT      10
    (both CoreML, in-process, no Python)

THE ENGINES SWAP, AND THAT IS THE RESULT
-----------------------------------------
WhisperKit is better on clean read speech by 0.27 points. Parakeet is better on the noisy and
accented split by 0.44 points. Neither dominates, and overall they are within 0.09 points of each
other on 105 913 words.

This is the strongest evidence yet for the plan's two-engine vote, and it is evidence of a kind
the reel could not provide. Two engines are a quality gate because their errors are UNCORRELATED,
not because there are two of them. An engine that merely lost everywhere would add nothing but
cost; two engines whose ranking INVERTS with recording conditions disagree precisely where the
material is hard, which is exactly where a transcript needs a confidence signal.

It also retires the last argument for Apple as the second vote: Apple measured 5.30% on the
synthetic set where these two measured 0.76%, and it makes the same meaning-inverting error
WhisperKit makes ("did" for "didn't"). A second engine must fail differently, not merely exist.

Cost
----
Parakeet is 12x faster (92-99x realtime against 7-8x) and 0.14 GB against ~1.6 GB. It also failed
on 1 utterance of 5559 against WhisperKit's 10. WhisperKit remains the primary because it supplies
per-word probabilities and verbatim disfluencies; Parakeet is the independent second opinion. On
these numbers the pairing costs about 8x realtime, dominated entirely by WhisperKit.

What this does NOT settle
-------------------------
LibriSpeech is read audiobook speech. It is real, unscripted in the sense that matters for
acoustics, and 200x more evidence than a TTS splice — but it is not conversational video, and the
absolute numbers will be worse on real rooms with real overlap. The RANKING and the SWAP are what
transfer. Long-form corpora closer to this tool's material (TED-LIUM, Earnings-21) are the next
measurement, not a substitute for this one.

Reproduce:
    curl -LO https://www.openslr.org/resources/12/test-clean.tar.gz && tar xzf test-clean.tar.gz
    sharpy transcribe-batch LibriSpeech/test-clean --out hyp --engine parakeet
    python bench/score_wer.py --ref LibriSpeech/test-clean --hyp hyp --name parakeet
