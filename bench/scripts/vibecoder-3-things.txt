[hook- that makes the echo]Every vibecoder must know these 3 things.

[1st cut - top]First folders.

[2nd cut- center]Second: instruction files.

[3rd cut- bottom]Third: hooks.

[4th cut- top]
This sounds basic, but it makes a huge difference.
Don't dump everything into random folders and expect your AI to figure it out.
Keep your components, services, tests, docs, and assets organized.

[5th cut - center]
CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md.
This is where you tell your agent what it needs to remember.
Your stack.
Your key rules.
How to run and test the project.
Important constraints.

[6th cut - bottom]
This is probably the most underused one.
If there's something you keep telling your agent to do...
Stop telling it.
Automate it with a hook.

[7th cut - top]
Your codebase is context for your agent.
Make it easy to understand.

[8th cut - center]
But don't write a 1,000-line instruction manual.
Keep it precise.
Put the things it needs every time in there.
For everything else, point it to deeper documentation.
Think of it as a map, not the whole encyclopedia.

[9th cut - botom]
Run tests.
Format code.
Validate changes.
Check files.
If you keep asking your agent to do the same thing, automate it.

[10th cut - top]
The best vibecoding setup isn't about writing better prompts.

[11th cut - center]
It's about giving your agent the right context.

[12th cut - botom]
And making the boring stuff happen automatically.

[13th cut - center]
Follow for more practical AI coding tips.
